### 1. Planning for resilience

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #能源转型 #多边治理

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: 欧盟委员会即将推出的Climate Resilience and Risk Management Integrated Framework不应只把热浪视为应急管理问题，而要把电力系统韧性、适应融资与监管协调纳入同一框架，否则成员国会在分散应对中放大脆弱性并错失制度化窗口。

The European Commission’s forthcoming Climate Resilience and Risk Management Integrated Framework should treat heatwaves not merely as emergency management, but as a single governance problem linking power-system resilience, adaptation finance, and regulatory coordination; otherwise member states will deepen vulnerability through fragmented responses and miss a window for institutionalization.

The argument is timed to Europe’s autumn 2026 legislative agenda, so the policy window is already open. It responds to the recent extreme summer and tries to turn seasonal crisis into the next round of institutional design.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 这是对欧盟即将进入立法定案阶段的适应与电力安全议程的前置干预。

This is an advance intervention in the EU’s adaptation and energy-security agenda as it moves toward legislative drafting and closure.

### Global climate adaptation governance: Why is it not legally binding?

Nina Hall, Åsa Persson · 2017-09-08 · European Journal of International Relations

#国际治理与多边主义 #地缘政治与治理

文章解释为何联合国气候框架下的适应治理长期保持低精度、低义务：适应被视为有争议的全球公共品，同时又常被与减缓承诺打包交易，结果是规则不少，但法律化程度有限。它的贡献在于把适应治理从“缺位”改写为“存在但弱约束”，并提示这种弱约束本身就是政治妥协的产物。

The article explains why adaptation governance under the UN climate framework remains low in precision and obligation: adaptation is treated as a contested global public good and is often bundled with mitigation commitments, producing many rules but limited legalization. Its contribution is to recast adaptation governance not as absent but as present with weak constraint, and to show that this weakness is itself the product of political compromise.

**方法论 / Methodology**

文章通过对UNFCCC主要文件、二手文献、专家与谈判者访谈，以及在气候谈判中的参与式观察，分析适应相关决定的精度与义务程度。

The article examines the precision and obligation of adaptation-related decisions through analysis of primary UNFCCC documents, secondary literature, expert and negotiator interviews, and participant observation at climate negotiations.

可与本期 E3G 的《Planning for resilience》并读：两者都在追问适应如何从临时应对进入制度化安排。

Read alongside E3G’s "Planning for resilience": both ask how adaptation moves from ad hoc response into institutionalized governance.

### Conceptualizing Climate Governance Beyond the International Regime

Chukwumerije Okereke, Harriet Bulkeley, Heike Schroeder · 2009-02-01 · Global Environmental Politics

#环境治理与气候 #多边治理

文章主张气候治理不能只用国际制度（international regime）来理解，而应把国家以下层级、私营部门和跨层级行动者纳入分析，因为气候政策实际上由多中心、多层级的治理拼接而成。它强调权力、公共—私营权威关系以及治理理性与实践的交织。

The article argues that climate governance cannot be understood only through the lens of the international regime; subnational, private-sector, and cross-level actors must be included because climate policy is assembled through polycentric, multi-level governance. It emphasizes power, public-private authority relations, and the interaction of governing rationalities and practices.

**方法论 / Methodology**

文章采用概念综述与理论整合的方法，将新葛兰西主义和治理术（governmentality）视角结合起来重构气候治理分析框架。

The article uses conceptual review and theoretical synthesis, combining neo-Gramscian and governmentality perspectives to rebuild the analytical frame for climate governance.

可与本期 Inside Climate News 的《Will a Data Center Join the Philadelphia Bellwether District’s Mix of Manufacturers and Warehouses?》同读：工业区再开发、数据中心选址与地方能源治理正体现多中心气候治理。

Read alongside Inside Climate News’ "Will a Data Center Join the Philadelphia Bellwether District’s Mix of Manufacturers and Warehouses?": industrial redevelopment, data-center siting, and local energy governance all exemplify polycentric climate governance.

**研究方向 / Research Directions**

  How does polycentric climate governance distribute power?
- 私权威如何进入气候政策？ (private authority, governmentality, climate policy, global governance)
  How does private authority enter climate policy?
