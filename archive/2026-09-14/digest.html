2. Climate and the task for leaders at UNGA81

E3G · 2026-09-08

#环境治理与气候#能源转型#多边治理

核心论点 / Core Argument

E3G 认为，UNGA81 期间各国领导人必须把气候行动重新表述为安全、负担能力、稳定与增长的政策工具，而不是把它留在单独的环境议题轨道上；其隐含主张是通过把清洁能源转型嵌入更广泛的危机管理框架，来维持跨议题联盟并抵消地缘政治紧张、经济压力和气候冲击叠加造成的议程碎片化。

E3G argues that leaders at UNGA81 must reframe climate action as a tool for security, affordability, stability, and growth rather than keeping it on a separate environmental track; the implication is that embedding the clean-energy transition in a broader crisis-management frame can sustain cross-issue coalitions and counter agenda fragmentation caused by geopolitical tension, economic pressure, and climate shocks.

这篇文章对应联合国大会第81届和纽约气候周的议程窗口，试图在高层政治聚集时重申气候与发展、能源和安全的联动。它也反映出在多重危机叠加下，倡议方希望抢占领导层话语。

3. El Niño: Indonesia fire emissions in 2026 ‘on track’ to match record for this century

Carbon Brief · 2026-09-11

#环境治理与气候#生物多样性#多边治理

核心论点 / Core Argument

Carbon Brief 指出，印度尼西亚火灾排放在厄尔尼诺（El Niño）驱动下再次逼近本世纪纪录，说明单靠年度火灾应对不足以处理泥炭地、土地利用和跨境烟霾的结构性风险；文章暗示需要把消防、土地治理、排放核算和区域协作纳入更长期的森林与气候政策框架。

Carbon Brief shows that Indonesia’s fire emissions, driven by El Niño, are again approaching this century’s record, demonstrating that annual firefighting is insufficient for the structural risks of peatlands, land use, and transboundary haze; the article implies that fire response, land governance, emissions accounting, and regional cooperation need to be built into a longer-term forest-and-climate policy framework.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

该文之所以在此时重要，是因为2026年的火季正在展开，且当前厄尔尼诺预计将持续到2027年，意味着排放与烟霾风险可能延续。它把短期灾害与中期政策设计直接连起来。

The piece matters now because the 2026 fire season is unfolding while the current El Niño is expected to last into 2027, implying that emissions and haze risks may persist. It links short-term disaster dynamics to medium-term policy design.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

处于印尼火灾季与区域烟霾治理的交汇处，也牵动全球碳排放与森林治理议程。

It sits at the intersection of Indonesia’s fire season and regional haze governance, while also affecting the global emissions and forest-governance agenda.

Between Mandate and Motivation

Mareike Well, Barbara Saerbeck, Helge Jörgens, Nina Kolleck · 2020-04-09 · Global Governance: A Review of Multilateralism and International Organizations

#多边治理#国际治理与多边主义

文章说明，UNFCCC 秘书处虽然受限于技术官僚式授权，却会在议程启动阶段采取更具企业家精神（entrepreneurial）的行政风格，在政策形成阶段也保持谨慎的推动姿态。它的核心关切不是秘书处是否“中立”，而是国际公共行政（international public administration）如何在严格授权下仍通过设题、促成协商与维持雄心，实际影响多边气候谈判的产出。

The article shows that, despite a tightly constrained technocratic mandate, the UNFCCC Secretariat acts in an entrepreneurial style at agenda-initiation stage and remains cautiously entrepreneurial during policy formulation. Its core concern is not whether the Secretariat is “neutral,” but how international public administration can still shape multilateral climate outcomes by setting issues, facilitating bargaining, and sustaining ambition under strict mandate constraints.

方法论 / Methodology

作者通过比较 UNFCCC 秘书处的行政风格，结合多边谈判过程分析来归纳理想型。

The authors compare the Secretariat’s administrative styles and use negotiation-process analysis to derive ideal types.

Regional Governance of Global Climate Change: The North American Commission for Environmental Cooperation

Michele M. Betsill · 2007-05-01 · Global Environmental Politics

#环境治理与气候#多边治理

文章评估北美环境合作委员会（CEC）作为区域气候治理场域的潜力，指出区域组织的优势通常被寄托于行动者数量较少、议题联结机会和国家—全球治理衔接，但这些优势在实践中都相当有限。它因此主张，区域气候治理不能被当成全球治理的自然补充，而要放进更大的多层级治理结构中考察其与其他机制的互动。

The article evaluates the North American Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC) as a site of regional climate governance, arguing that the usual advantages of regional organizations—fewer actors, opportunities for issue linkage, and linkage between national and global governance systems—are limited in practice. It therefore insists that regional climate governance should not be treated as a natural supplement to global governance, but analyzed in terms of how it interacts with other mechanisms in a larger multilevel structure.

方法论 / Methodology

文章采用区域制度的比较制度分析，围绕三种潜在优势评估 CEC。

The article uses comparative institutional analysis of a regional body against three hypothesized advantages.
