研究动向 / Research Signals

1. How climate finance can deliver in a multipolar world

E3G · 2026-08-18

#综合治理议题#能源转型#多边治理

核心论点 / Core Argument

E3G认为，在地缘政治碎片化、利率高企和发展援助收缩的环境下，气候融资必须从单一的资金动员转向以多边开发银行、债务工具和风险分担机制为核心的组合，以避免发展中国家在转型中被融资约束排除在外。

E3G argues that amid geopolitical fragmentation, high interest rates, and shrinking development assistance, climate finance must shift from simple capital mobilization to a package centered on multilateral development banks, debt instruments, and risk-sharing mechanisms, or developing countries will be priced out of the transition.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

这篇文章回应的是多边合作弱化与财政空间收缩同步发生的时点。它把气候融资从一般性倡议推进到“如何在碎片化世界里仍能交付”的操作层面。

The piece responds to a moment when multilateral cooperation is weakening as fiscal space tightens. It moves climate finance from a general commitment to the operational question of delivery in a fragmented world.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

它位于气候融资、债务可持续性与发展筹资的交叉议程上，并直接指向多边开发银行改革与COP前后的资金动员安排。

It sits at the intersection of climate finance, debt sustainability, and development finance, with direct implications for MDB reform and pre-COP mobilization arrangements.

2. Q&A: What is ‘long-duration energy storage’ – and why does the UK need it?

Carbon Brief · 2026-08-19

#环境治理与气候#绿色转型#能源转型

核心论点 / Core Argument

Carbon Brief将长时储能（long-duration energy storage）界定为支撑英国风光系统从“小时级平衡”走向“日到周级调节”的关键基础设施，Ofgem的cap-and-floor机制则是把市场无法充分定价的系统灵活性变成可融资资产，从而降低整个电力系统成本并缓解弃风弃光风险。

Carbon Brief frames long-duration energy storage as the infrastructure that allows the UK to move from hour-by-hour balancing toward day-to-week flexibility in a wind-and-solar system, while Ofgem’s cap-and-floor scheme turns underpriced system flexibility into a financeable asset that can lower system costs and reduce curtailment risk.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

这篇Q&A紧接Ofgem已识别16个待支持项目的节点，说明储能已从概念进入监管选择阶段。它把讨论从技术介绍推进到监管设计与投资激励。

The Q&A comes just as Ofgem has identified 16 projects for potential support, showing that storage has moved from concept to regulatory selection. It shifts the discussion from technology primer to regulatory design and investment incentives.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

它位于英国电力市场改革、系统灵活性配置和净零基础设施投资框架之内。

It sits within UK electricity-market reform, system-flexibility allocation, and the investment framework for net-zero infrastructure.

Several items converge on one question: the transition is not about whether to act, but who bears the costs and who writes the rules. Oil-and-gas approvals, storage investment, and climate finance all expose the tension between security, industrial policy, and emissions constraints.

论文研读 / Research Reading

Inter-organizational Relations and Policy Coordination

Adam Kamradt-Scott · 2025-12-16 · Global Governance: A Review of Multilateralism and International Organizations

#多边治理#国际治理与多边主义

文章聚焦国际组织之间的政策协调，指出在成员国面对国际组织增殖时，推动机构协同已成为减少竞争、冲突和重叠的常见治理回应。作者认为“政策协调”在既有文献中概念化不足，并以四方机制（Quadripartite）应对抗微生物药物耐药性为例，检验其协调效果及国家层面的行动计划扩散。

The article focuses on policy coordination among international organizations and argues that, as member states face proliferating IOs, encouraging institutional collaboration has become a common governance response to reduce competition, conflict, and overlap. It also contends that ‘policy coordination’ remains underconceptualized in the literature and examines the Quadripartite’s efforts on antimicrobial resistance through national action plan adoption.

方法论 / Methodology

文章通过概念讨论结合对四方机制协调效果的案例分析，围绕成员国国家行动计划的扩散进行评估。

The article combines conceptual discussion with a case analysis of the Quadripartite’s coordination efforts, evaluating the diffusion of member states’ national action plans.
