Paris Agreement climate proposals need a boost to keep warming well below 2 °C

Rogelj et al. · 2016 · paper

#NDC

核心观点 / Argument

文章认为，现有国家承诺不足以把升温稳定在远低于 2°C，更不足以支撑1.5°C目标；巴黎机制的关键不只是提交承诺，而是后续持续加码。

The paper argues that existing national pledges are insufficient for holding warming well below 2°C, let alone 1.5°C; the Paris mechanism depends on repeated strengthening of pledges.

方法 / Method

作者汇总各国减排承诺，将其转化为全球排放路径，并与多种温控情景进行比较。

The authors aggregate national mitigation pledges, translate them into global emissions pathways, and compare them with temperature-consistent scenarios.

例子或证据 / Evidence

核心证据是INDC/NDC承诺所对应的2030年排放水平，与2°C和1.5°C路径之间仍存在显著缺口。

The key evidence is the gap between projected 2030 emissions under INDC/NDC pledges and pathways compatible with 2°C and 1.5°C.

今日关联 / Relevance

适合解读任何关于NDC更新、全球盘点或气候雄心不足的新闻。

Useful for interpreting news on NDC updates, the global stocktake, and ambition gaps.

Debt sustainability in developing countries

Wyplosz · 2011 · paper

#债务可持续性

核心观点 / Argument

文章认为，债务可持续性不是单一阈值问题，而取决于增长、利率、汇率、财政能力和外部冲击之间的动态关系。

The paper argues that debt sustainability is not a single-threshold problem but depends on growth, interest rates, exchange rates, fiscal capacity, and external shocks.

方法 / Method

采用宏观财政分析和债务动态框架，讨论债务存量、偿债能力和危机风险。

It uses macro-fiscal analysis and debt dynamics to discuss debt stocks, repayment capacity, and crisis risk.

例子或证据 / Evidence

发展中国家的增长波动、外币债务和外部融资条件被用来说明债务风险为何容易被低估。

Growth volatility, foreign-currency debt, and external financing conditions illustrate why debt risks in developing countries can be underestimated.

今日关联 / Relevance

适合分析气候融资贷款化、灾后重建融资和发展中国家财政空间压力。

Useful for assessing loan-heavy climate finance, post-disaster reconstruction finance, and fiscal-space pressure in developing countries.
