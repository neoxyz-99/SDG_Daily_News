今日深读 / Reading List · 3

A green industrial policy for Europe

Rodrik · 2014 · paper

#绿色转型

核心观点 / Argument

文章认为，绿色技术和产业转型面临创新外部性、协调失败和市场不确定性，因此政府需要通过产业政策主动塑造投资方向。

The paper argues that green technologies face innovation externalities, coordination failures, and uncertainty, requiring governments to shape investment through industrial policy.

方法 / Method

以理论经济学和政策分析为主，讨论绿色产业政策的理由、工具和治理风险。

It uses economic theory and policy analysis to examine the rationale, tools, and governance risks of green industrial policy.

例子或证据 / Evidence

文章讨论补贴、公共研发、采购、标准和部门合作等工具，并强调政策设计需要避免寻租和低效保护。

It discusses subsidies, public R&D, procurement, standards, and sectoral coordination while warning against rent-seeking and inefficient protection.

今日关联 / Relevance

适合分析欧盟 CBAM、绿色产业补贴、清洁技术供应链和转型竞争政策。

Useful for interpreting the EU CBAM, green subsidies, clean-tech supply chains, and transition competitiveness policy.

Mission-oriented innovation policy and dynamic capabilities in the public sector

Kattel and Mazzucato · 2018 · paper

#绿色转型

核心观点 / Argument

文章认为，应对气候和发展挑战需要公共部门具备设定任务、协调市场、学习调整和承担风险的动态能力。

The paper argues that climate and development challenges require public agencies with dynamic capabilities to set missions, coordinate markets, learn, and take risks.

方法 / Method

通过创新理论、公共管理和制度能力分析，提出公共部门能力建设框架。

It draws on innovation theory, public management, and institutional-capability analysis to build a public-sector capability framework.

例子或证据 / Evidence

文章参考任务导向政策和公共投资实践，说明政府不仅是修补市场失败者，也可以是市场塑造者。

It draws on mission-oriented policy and public investment practice to show that governments can shape markets, not merely fix failures.

今日关联 / Relevance

适合解读绿色转型中政府如何设计产业任务、公共投资和跨部门协调。

Useful for reading news about industrial missions, public investment, and cross-sector coordination in green transition.

A climate finance accounting framework

Roberts et al. · 2021 · paper

#气候金融

核心观点 / Argument

文章认为，气候资金争议的核心在于口径不清：贷款、赠款、私营动员资金和重新标记的发展援助不能被简单相加，否则会夸大实际支持。

The article argues that disputes over climate finance stem from unclear accounting: loans, grants, private mobilization, and relabelled aid cannot be treated as equivalent support.

方法 / Method

通过规范性核算框架区分资金来源、工具、赠款等价额、额外性和受援方实际获得的净支持。

It develops a normative accounting framework distinguishing sources, instruments, grant-equivalent value, additionality, and net support received.

例子或证据 / Evidence

文章以发达国家气候资金承诺和 1000 亿美元目标的核算争议为背景，说明不同口径会得出截然不同的资金规模。

Using debates around developed-country finance pledges and the USD 100 billion goal, it shows how accounting choices can radically change reported finance levels.

今日关联 / Relevance

适合分析 MDB 气候融资、适应资金、损失与损害资金以及绿色贷款是否真正增加发展中国家财政空间。

Useful for assessing whether MDB climate finance, adaptation finance, loss-and-damage finance, or green lending genuinely expands fiscal space.
