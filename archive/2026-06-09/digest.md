### 1. Scaling Climate Action by Lowering Emissions (SCALE)

- 来源 / Source: World Bank Climate
- 日期 / Date: 2026-06-09
- 标签 / Tags: #气候金融 #债务可持续性 #Global South
- SDG links: #SDG13, /sdg-17/
- 原文 / Original: https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/scale/

**中文摘要**: SCALE是一个多捐助者基金，旨在通过实施基于成果的气候融资，推动变革性的气候行动，并帮助各国访问国际碳市场，从而缓解融资差距。

**English brief**: SCALE is a multi-donor fund aiming to catalyze transformative climate action through Results-Based Climate Finance, helping countries access international carbon markets to bridge financial gaps.

**为什么重要**: 此项目的成功实施将有助于降低碳排放，并促进全球气候融资，从而推动实现气候可持续和经济发展的双重目标。

**Why it matters**: The successful implementation of this initiative will help lower carbon emissions and promote global climate financing, thus advancing both climate sustainability and economic development.

**关键词/术语 / Terms**

- Results-Based Climate Finance / 以成果为基础的气候融资: 以成果为基础的气候融资是一种将资金支付与实现特定气候成果相挂钩的融资模式。
- Carbon Markets / 碳市场: 碳市场是一个用于交易碳排放配额的市场，旨在通过市场机制促进减排。

### Paris Agreement climate proposals need a boost to keep warming well below 2 °C [#NDC]

Rogelj et al. (2016) · paper

- 核心观点 / Argument: 文章认为，现有国家承诺不足以把升温稳定在远低于 2°C，更不足以支撑1.5°C目标；巴黎机制的关键不只是提交承诺，而是后续持续加码。 / The paper argues that existing national pledges are insufficient for holding warming well below 2°C, let alone 1.5°C; the Paris mechanism depends on repeated strengthening of pledges.
- 方法 / Method: 作者汇总各国减排承诺，将其转化为全球排放路径，并与多种温控情景进行比较。 / The authors aggregate national mitigation pledges, translate them into global emissions pathways, and compare them with temperature-consistent scenarios.
- 例子或证据 / Evidence: 核心证据是INDC/NDC承诺所对应的2030年排放水平，与2°C和1.5°C路径之间仍存在显著缺口。 / The key evidence is the gap between projected 2030 emissions under INDC/NDC pledges and pathways compatible with 2°C and 1.5°C.
- 今日关联 / Relevance: 适合解读任何关于NDC更新、全球盘点或气候雄心不足的新闻。 / Useful for interpreting news on NDC updates, the global stocktake, and ambition gaps.
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1038/nature18307

### Debt sustainability in developing countries [#债务可持续性]

Wyplosz (2011) · paper

- 核心观点 / Argument: 文章认为，债务可持续性不是单一阈值问题，而取决于增长、利率、汇率、财政能力和外部冲击之间的动态关系。 / The paper argues that debt sustainability is not a single-threshold problem but depends on growth, interest rates, exchange rates, fiscal capacity, and external shocks.
- 方法 / Method: 采用宏观财政分析和债务动态框架，讨论债务存量、偿债能力和危机风险。 / It uses macro-fiscal analysis and debt dynamics to discuss debt stocks, repayment capacity, and crisis risk.
- 例子或证据 / Evidence: 发展中国家的增长波动、外币债务和外部融资条件被用来说明债务风险为何容易被低估。 / Growth volatility, foreign-currency debt, and external financing conditions illustrate why debt risks in developing countries can be underestimated.
- 今日关联 / Relevance: 适合分析气候融资贷款化、灾后重建融资和发展中国家财政空间压力。 / Useful for assessing loan-heavy climate finance, post-disaster reconstruction finance, and fiscal-space pressure in developing countries.
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1093/oxrep/grr007
