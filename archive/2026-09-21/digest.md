### 1. Analysis: India’s power-sector emissions flat for two years due to clean-energy surge

Carbon Brief · 2026-09-16

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #能源转型 #NDC

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: 印度电力部门在需求继续上升的情况下实现两年排放持平，说明太阳能与其他清洁电力已开始接住增量负荷，但煤电新增装机仍通过更低利用小时推高系统成本；文章隐含的政策含义是，电力治理不能只追求装机扩张，而要同步压低煤电边际运行并处理钢铁、水泥等难减排部门的增量来源。

India’s power sector kept emissions flat for two years even as demand rose, showing that solar and other clean power can now absorb incremental load, but new coal capacity still raises system costs through lower utilization hours; the policy implication is that power governance must go beyond capacity expansion to curb marginal coal generation while tackling rising emissions from steel, cement, and other hard-to-abate sectors.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 这篇分析对应的是2026年上半年的最新六个月数据，正好检验印度清洁电力扩张是否已从“增量补位”转向“系统重塑”。它也回应了能源安全与减排之间的再平衡问题。

The piece uses the latest first-half-2026 data to test whether India’s clean-power expansion has moved from filling incremental demand to reshaping the system. It also speaks to the rebalancing between energy security and emissions reduction.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 它处在印度电力规划、煤电新增审批和后续NDC执行的交汇处。

It sits at the intersection of India’s power planning, new coal approvals, and future NDC implementation.

Original: https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-indias-power-sector-emissions-flat-for-two-years-due-to-clean-energy-surge

Carbon Brief · 2026-09-21

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #粮食与土地 #水资源治理

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: 当前厄尔尼诺已逼近历史极值，说明气候系统内部变率正在与持续升温叠加，从而把短期天气冲击放大为跨区域的粮食、能源和灾害管理压力；文章提示政策上不能把厄尔尼诺仅视为自然背景噪音，而应把它纳入应急预算、预报体系和跨部门风险治理。

The current El Niño is approaching historic extremes, showing how internal climate variability is stacking with long-term warming to amplify short-term shocks into cross-border pressures on food, energy, and disaster management; the article implies that El Niño should be treated not as background noise but as a trigger for contingency budgeting, forecasting systems, and cross-sector risk governance.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 这篇文章之所以现在重要，是因为它用9月中旬的海温记录判断本轮事件仍在加速，且强厄尔尼诺通常在年底前后达到峰值。它为接下来数月的灾害预警和财政准备提供了时间窗口。

This matters now because the mid-September sea-surface record suggests the event is still intensifying, and strong El Niño episodes usually peak toward year-end. That creates a short window for disaster warning and fiscal preparedness.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 它对应的是各国气象预警、粮食安全和灾害资金的季节性调度，而不是单一国际谈判议程。

## 论文研读 / Research Reading

### Mainstreaming adaptation to climate change in Least Developed Countries (LDCs)

Saleemul Huq, Hannah Reid, Mama Konate, Atiq Rahman, Youba Sokona and Florence Crick · 2004 · Climate Policy

#Global South #发展不平等

Huq 等人以孟加拉国和马里为例，讨论气候适应为何尚未充分进入最不发达国家的日常政策过程。两国已经积累了灾害应对、水资源管理和农业气象服务经验，但识别出适应措施并不等于将其纳入国家决策。文章强调，需要直接面向政策制定者，将科学研究转化为适合政策使用的语言与时间尺度。

Huq and colleagues examine why adaptation remains weakly integrated into national policy in least developed countries. Drawing on Bangladesh and Mali, they describe existing disaster-response, water-management, and agricultural weather services, while identifying a gap between practical adaptation options and routine policymaking. Their recommendation is to engage policymakers and translate scientific findings into language and timescales that fit policy decisions.

**方法论 / Methodology**

文章围绕孟加拉国与马里的适应实践展开政策讨论；摘要说明了适应措施与政策整合障碍，未交代因果效应估计设计。

A policy discussion based on Bangladesh and Mali; the abstract describes adaptation practices and barriers to policy integration, without specifying a causal estimation design.

**今日关联 / Today's Connection**

结合本期尼泊尔气候灾害索赔报道，可进一步追问：国际层面对脆弱性的关注如何转化为国家政策中的适应行动？这篇论文并不研究损失与损害赔偿。

Alongside this issue’s report on Nepal’s climate-disaster claims, this paper raises a related question: how can international attention to vulnerability translate into adaptation within national policy? It does not examine loss-and-damage compensation.

  How do adaptation measures enter national policy?
- 气候研究如何适配政策时间表？ (science-policy interface, adaptation, policy timing)
  How can climate research fit policy timescales?

### Producing the Climate: States, Scientists, and the Constitution of Global Governance Objects

Bentley B. Allan · 2016-12-20 · International Organization

#国际治理与多边主义 #多边治理

这篇文章指出，气候并不是一个天然固定的治理对象，而是在国家机构、科学共同体与国际组织的互动中被“做成”当前这种以地球物理过程为中心的对象。作者强调，治理议题的边界会随着国家需求与科学知识的共生产而变化，二氧化碳、碳汇和土地利用等内容被纳入气候框架，正体现了这种过程。它的价值在于提醒研究者：争论不只是如何治理气候，而是气候究竟被定义成什么。

This article argues that climate is not a fixed governance object but something produced through interaction among state agencies, scientists, and international organizations, which shaped climate into a geophysical object. The author shows how the issue boundary shifts through co-production of state demand and scientific knowledge, as carbon sinks and land use entered the climate frame. Its value is to remind researchers that the dispute is not only how to govern climate, but what climate is defined to be.

**方法论 / Methodology**

文章结合科学技术研究（STS）与全球治理的概念分析，并以历史过程追踪国家—科学互动。它是解释性研究，不以单一计量模型为核心。

The article combines science and technology studies with conceptual analysis of global governance, using historical process tracing of state-science interaction. It is an interpretive study rather than a single econometric model.

**今日关联 / Today's Connection**

可与本期 Carbon Brief 的《Analysis: India’s power-sector emissions flat for two years due to clean-energy surge》一起读，因为后者显示电力转型已不只是减排问题，也在重塑治理对象本身。

Read alongside Carbon Brief’s “Analysis: India’s power-sector emissions flat for two years due to clean-energy surge,” since it shows how power transition is reshaping the governance object itself, not just emissions outcomes.

**研究方向 / Research Directions**

- 气候对象如何被重新定义？ (governance objects, science-policy, climate politics, co-production)
  How are climate objects redefined?
- 国家如何塑造科学议程？ (state-science relations, epistemic communities, climate knowledge, policy framing)
  How do states shape scientific agendas?

DOI / 原文链接: https://doi.org/10.1017/s0020818316000321
