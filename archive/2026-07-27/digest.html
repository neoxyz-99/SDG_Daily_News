1. Factcheck: No, Europe’s heatwaves are not being ‘caused’ by declining air pollution

Carbon Brief · 2026-07-24

#环境治理与气候#绿色转型

核心论点 / Core Argument

欧洲热浪并非由减污本身“造成”，真正主因仍是温室气体驱动的增暖；空气污染下降只是削弱了过去对变暖的遮蔽效应，因而政策争论不应把净零转型当作升温元凶，而应把减排、减污与公共沟通重新分层。

Europe’s heatwaves are not being ‘caused’ by cleaner air; greenhouse-gas-driven warming remains the main driver, while falling pollution only removes part of the masking effect. The policy implication is that climate mitigation, air-quality policy, and public communication must be separated rather than blamed on net-zero transition.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

这一澄清直接回应了本周欧洲热浪中的舆论误读，也回应了将极端高温反向归咎于减排政策的政治叙事。它把争论从单一污染变量拉回到长期温室气体累积。

This directly answers the current misreading of Europe’s heatwaves and the political move to blame mitigation policy for rising temperatures. It shifts the debate back to long-run greenhouse-gas accumulation.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

It sits at the intersection of Europe’s climate narrative and net-zero politics, shaping who gets to interpret extreme weather and with what policy legitimacy.

The analysis matches the timing of unusual warming in the first half of 2026 and concurrent European heatwaves, explaining how short-term ocean-atmosphere dynamics amplify an already elevated baseline. It also provides a temporal anchor for risk expectations into late 2026 and 2027.

经典研读 / Classic Reading

Structural Causes and Regime Consequences: Regimes as Intervening Variables

Stephen D. Krasner · 1982 · International Organization

#多边治理

Krasner 追问国际制度是否只是权力分布的副产品，还是能在国家利益之间形成相对稳定的约束。他将 regime 界定为特定议题领域中的原则、规范、规则和决策程序，并强调制度不是单纯的组织外壳，而是通过塑造合法性、预期和可接受行为边界来影响合作。文章的贡献在于把“权力决定规则”和“规则反过来组织权力”放进同一分析框架，适合解释气候谈判、发展融资和多边机构改革中为何同一承诺在不同制度安排下会产生不同约束力。

Krasner asks whether international regimes are merely by-products of power or whether they can independently structure state behavior. By defining regimes as principles, norms, rules, and decision-making procedures around specific issue areas, the article shows how institutions shape legitimacy, expectations, and the boundaries of acceptable cooperation. Its lasting contribution is to hold together two claims often treated separately: power creates rules, but rules also organize how power is exercised. This makes the article useful for reading climate negotiations, development finance, and institutional reform as contests over the force and meaning of commitments.

方法论 / Methodology

文章采用概念建构和理论综述方法，通过梳理不同国际关系理论对 regime 的解释，界定制度变量能解释什么。其优势是提供清晰分析语言，限制是缺少系统经验检验。

International Regimes, Transactions, and Change: Embedded Liberalism in the Postwar Economic Order

John Gerard Ruggie · 1982 · International Organization

#多边治理#发展不平等#绿色转型

Ruggie 解释战后经济秩序为何没有简单回到自由放任，而是把开放贸易和资本流动嵌入国内就业、福利和产业调节承诺之中。他提出“嵌入式自由主义”，指出国际经济规则之所以能够稳定运行，是因为它在外部开放与内部社会保护之间建立了政治交换。文章的分析价值在于提醒读者，所谓开放秩序从来不是纯市场安排，而是依赖国家能力和社会妥协来维持合法性；这对理解绿色产业政策、气候融资条件和发展中国家政策空间争议尤其有用。

Ruggie explains why the postwar economic order did not simply restore laissez-faire liberalism, but embedded open markets within domestic commitments to employment, welfare, and policy space. The concept of embedded liberalism shows that international economic rules become durable only when external openness is balanced by domestic social protection. The article matters because it treats the open order as a political settlement, not a neutral market arrangement. It is especially useful for interpreting green industrial policy, climate finance conditionality, and disputes over the development space available to poorer countries.

方法论 / Methodology

文章采用历史制度分析和概念史方法，把战后经济秩序放入权力、规则和国内社会契约的互动中解释。它能揭示制度安排的政治基础，但不提供可量化的因果估计。

Coding Error or Statistical Embellishment? The Political Economy of Reporting Climate Aid

Axel Michaelowa and Katharina Michaelowa · 2011 · World Development

#气候金融#发展不平等#多边治理

Michaelowa 与 Michaelowa 研究气候援助统计为何会系统性膨胀，核心问题是捐助国如何借助援助标记规则，把原本主要服务发展目标的项目包装为气候相关资金。文章指出，气候金融数字并非中性事实，而是由报告激励、国际声誉竞争和模糊分类标准共同生产出来。其贡献在于把“资金承诺”转化为可审查的政治经济问题：谁有动力高估气候贡献，哪些项目真正具有额外性，哪些只是既有援助的重新标注。这对阅读任何气候融资新闻都很关键。

Michaelowa and Michaelowa examine why reported climate aid may be systematically inflated. The core issue is how donor governments use aid-marking rules to classify projects with primarily development objectives as climate-related finance. The article shows that climate finance figures are not neutral facts; they are produced through reporting incentives, reputational competition, and ambiguous classification standards. Its contribution is to turn finance pledges into a political economy question: who benefits from over-reporting, which projects are genuinely additional, and which are repackaged development aid.

方法论 / Methodology

文章采用政治经济分析和援助数据核查方法，比较报告规则、捐助国激励和资金标记实践。它能揭示统计口径的偏差，但受限于公开援助数据的完整性。

今日关联 / Today's Connection

Read with E3G’s financing argument on EU Clean Trade Partnerships: once funding pledges become the basis of cooperation credibility, accounting rules and additionality disputes directly shape partner-country trust.
