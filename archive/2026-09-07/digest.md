### 1. Analysis: UK solar power hits record high over summer 2026

Carbon Brief · 2026-09-04

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #能源转型 #绿色转型

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: 英国太阳能发电在极端高温与长日照条件下创下夏季纪录，说明电力系统中的可再生能源部署已开始把气候冲击转化为供给增量；文章隐含的政策含义是，电网调度、储能和并网规则必须同步升级，才能把“天气红利”稳定转化为系统韧性，而不是只在短期内抬高发电峰值。

UK solar generation set a summer record under extreme heat and long daylight, showing that renewable deployment is beginning to turn climate stress into supply gains; the policy implication is that grid dispatch, storage, and interconnection rules must advance in parallel so weather-driven gains become system resilience rather than a short-lived output spike.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 这篇分析回应的是2026年夏季高温、日照异常和发电纪录同时出现的窗口，说明“更多太阳能”不再只是装机故事，而是电力系统适配极端天气的治理问题。它也把政策讨论从扩张速度推向系统整合。

The piece responds to the 2026 summer when record heat, unusual sunshine, and record output coincided, showing that ‘more solar’ is no longer just an installation story but a governance problem of system adaptation to extremes. It shifts the debate from capacity growth to system integration.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 它处在英国电力市场改革与净零执行框架的交汇处，直接触及并网、储能和需求侧响应的政策排序。

It sits at the intersection of UK power-market reform and net-zero implementation, directly affecting the policy sequencing of interconnection, storage, and demand-side response.

## 论文研读 / Research Reading

### Gatekeeping the Greenland ice sheet: climate change data and the “upstream” production of global governance objects

Lin Alexandra Mortensgaard · 2025-10-22 · European Journal of International Relations

#国际治理与多边主义 #地缘政治与治理

文章提出“守门”（gatekeeping）概念，强调全球治理对象不是在发表结果时才成形，而是在数据被采集、筛选和初步分析的上游阶段就已被权力关系塑造。作者通过格陵兰冰盖冰芯研究的跨点民族志，说明国家与科学机构如何控制原始气候数据的流动与内容，并把数据主权（data sovereignty）问题前移到知识生产的起点。

The article develops ‘gatekeeping’ to show that global governance objects are shaped not at the point of published results, but upstream when data are scoped, extracted, and preliminarily analyzed. Through multi-sited ethnography of Greenland ice-core science, the author shows how states and scientific institutions control the flow and content of raw climate data and moves data sovereignty to the start of knowledge production.

**方法论 / Methodology**

作者采用多点民族志（multi-sited ethnography），结合参与式观察与半结构式访谈，追踪格陵兰、德国和丹麦的冰芯科学实践。

The author uses multi-sited ethnography, combining participant observation and semi-structured interviews across Greenland, Germany, and Denmark, to trace ice-core science in practice.

**今日关联 / Today's Connection**

可与本期 Carbon Brief 关于英国太阳能纪录高位一文对读：两者都提示，能源与气候治理的关键不只在结果端，更在数据、基础设施和制度门槛如何被提前设定。

Read alongside this issue’s Carbon Brief piece on UK solar’s record output: both show that energy and climate governance are shaped not only at the results stage, but by how data, infrastructure, and institutional thresholds are set upstream.
