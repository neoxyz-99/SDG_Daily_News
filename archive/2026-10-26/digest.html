1. Chart: A record two-thirds of car sales in China are now electric vehicles

Carbon Brief · 2026-10-23

#环境治理与气候#能源转型#绿色转型

核心论点 / Core Argument

Carbon Brief 通过中国乘联会数据指出，2026年9月新能源车占新车销量67%，在油价上升和燃油车需求下滑的背景下，电动化已从政策扶持转向市场自我强化，并开始通过出口扩张影响全球汽车产业与石油需求。

Using China Passenger Car Association data, Carbon Brief shows that new-energy vehicles reached 67% of new car sales in September 2026, indicating that electrification is shifting from subsidy-led expansion toward self-reinforcing market growth and now affects global auto trade and oil demand through exports and transport substitution.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

这一变化发生在霍尔木兹危机推高燃油成本之后，说明外部能源冲击正在加速交通电动化，而不是只造成短期扰动。2026年9月的销量数据也为观察中国汽车市场结构转折提供了最新窗口。

The shift comes after the Hormuz crisis raised fuel costs, suggesting that external energy shocks are accelerating transport electrification rather than merely causing temporary disruption. The September 2026 sales data provide the latest window into a structural turn in China’s auto market.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

处于中国交通电动化、石油需求变化与汽车出口竞争三重议程的交汇点。

This sits at the intersection of China’s transport electrification agenda, changing oil demand, and export competition in the auto industry.

2. Experts: How are climate scientists using artificial intelligence?

Carbon Brief · 2026-10-22

#环境治理与气候#数字基础设施#多边治理

核心论点 / Core Argument

Carbon Brief 汇集多位专家意见指出，AI 已进入天气预测、卫星图像分析和气候政策支持等环节，但其算力消耗、结果可验证性不足及对科研不平等的放大效应，意味着科研机构需要同时建立验证标准、能耗约束和公平使用框架。

By assembling expert views, Carbon Brief shows that AI is now being used in weather prediction, satellite-image analysis and climate-policy support, but its energy demand, weak verifiability and tendency to widen scientific inequalities imply that research institutions need validation standards, energy constraints and equitable access rules at the same time.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

AI 热潮正在迅速进入气候科学工作流，若缺乏方法审查与资源分配规则，工具效能可能伴随新的偏差和不平等。本文及时回应了这一扩张窗口中的治理缺口。

The AI boom is rapidly entering climate-science workflows; without method checks and allocation rules, gains in performance may come with new bias and inequality. The piece responds to this governance gap at the moment of expansion.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

位于气候科学方法革新、科研治理与政策工具应用的交叉议程。

This sits at the intersection of climate-science methods, research governance and policy-tool deployment.

3. Climate may have slipped down the Annual Meetings agenda, but remains on the balance sheet

E3G · 2026-10-22

#环境治理与气候#能源转型#多边治理

核心论点 / Core Argument

E3G 认为，曼谷 IMF-世界银行年会表面上弱化了气候议题，但能源安全、极端天气与财政约束仍把气候风险留在国家资产负债表上，因此多边开发银行和融资工具必须把预防性适应投资与灾后快速支持纳入常规金融安排。

E3G argues that climate may have receded from the main stage at the Bangkok IMF-World Bank Annual Meetings, but energy insecurity, extreme weather and fiscal pressure still keep climate risk on governments’ balance sheets, so multilateral development banks and finance tools must embed preventive adaptation investment and rapid post-shock support in routine financial arrangements.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

这篇文章抓住年会窗口，说明气候不再只是议程文本中的高调话题，而是被财政和风险管理逻辑重新编码。它直接回应当前主权融资与适应投资的现实约束。

The piece uses the Annual Meetings window to show climate being recoded by fiscal and risk-management logic rather than just by agenda rhetoric. It responds directly to current constraints on sovereign finance and adaptation investment.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

处于IMF-世界银行年会、开发融资与气候风险主流化的交叉节点。

This sits at the intersection of the IMF-World Bank Annual Meetings, development finance and the mainstreaming of climate risk.

4. EU ETS – Foundation for a Green Steel Ecosystem

E3G · 2026-10-26

#环境治理与气候#能源转型#多边治理

核心论点 / Core Argument

E3G 认为，欧盟碳排放交易体系（EU ETS）只有与配套立法、需求侧信号和产业政策联动，才能把先锋企业的绿色钢铁计划转化为可融资、可复制的低碳产能，否则转型会停留在个别项目而难形成体系。

E3G argues that the EU Emissions Trading System can support green steel only if it is paired with complementary legislation, demand-side signals and industrial policy; otherwise pioneering projects will not become financeable, replicable low-carbon capacity and the transition will remain fragmented.

为什么此刻重要 / Why Now

欧洲正在把重工业减排从示范项目推进到体系构建阶段，这使得碳价、法规和市场培育的组合是否到位成为当下关键。文章把ETS从单一减排工具提升为产业生态的制度底座。

Europe is moving heavy-industry decarbonization from pilots to system-building, making the adequacy of the carbon-price, regulatory and market-building mix a live issue. The piece recasts the ETS as institutional foundation rather than a stand-alone abatement tool.

议程位置 / Agenda Position

This sits at the intersection of EU industrial decarbonization, carbon-market design and competitiveness policy.

论文研读 / Research Reading

Multilateral Climate Finance in Pursuit of Private Capital: How the De-Risking Agenda Is Changing International Organizations

Erin R. Graham, Jiayi (Sonia) Zhang · 2026-10-13 · Global Environmental Politics

#环境治理与气候#多边治理

本文指出，多边气候融资正在被“去风险化（de-risking）”议程重塑：国际组织越来越把多边资金用来撬动机构投资者和国内银行，而不是直接投向单个减缓或适应项目，这模糊了经济类与环境类国际组织的边界，也带来透明度问题。

The article argues that multilateral climate finance is being reshaped by a de-risking agenda: international organizations increasingly use multilateral funds to mobilize institutional investors and domestic banks rather than finance individual mitigation or adaptation projects, blurring the line between economic and environmental IOs and creating transparency problems.

方法论 / Methodology

作者通过对五个气候融资体制内国际组织的增资文件进行文本分析，并结合全球环境基金（GEF）与绿色气候基金（GCF）项目评估，比较去风险化如何改变资金用途与组织角色。

The authors combine text analysis of replenishment documents from five climate-finance international organizations with an assessment of GEF and GCF projects to compare how de-risking changes funding use and organizational roles.

#环境治理与气候#多边治理

本文主张，全球环境治理中的“准入”不能只理解为参会名额或开放注册；COP26 的协作事件民族志显示，歧视性设计、技术差异与认知不公会阻碍持续、有效的参与，并放大权力差距。

The article argues that access in global environmental governance cannot be reduced to attendance or open registration; collaborative event ethnography at COP26 shows that discriminatory design, technological disparities and recognitional injustice obstruct sustained, meaningful participation and deepen power asymmetries.

The authors use collaborative event ethnography at COP26 and draw on critical disability studies to rethink the differences among access, accessibility and inclusion.
