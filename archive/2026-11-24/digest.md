### 4. Water-governance resilience depends on coordination and accountability
Original title: Resilience as a governing principle in water governance

Nature Sustainability · 2026-11-17

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #可持续金融与ESG #水资源治理

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: 作者通过五个国际案例指出，水治理中的韧性（resilience）不应被当作静态终点，而应作为治理设计与运行原则，依赖适应性、协调、学习和问责来处理跨部门、跨尺度的复杂不确定性。

Drawing on five international cases, the authors argue that resilience in water governance should not be treated as a static end state but as a governing principle, relying on adaptability, coordination, learning and accountability to manage cross-sector and cross-scale uncertainty.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 随着旱涝风险和基础设施脆弱性上升，单纯强调工程能力已不足以解释水系统为何失灵或如何修复。该研究为当前围绕适应与执行的政策争论提供了更制度化的语言。

As drought and flood risks rise and infrastructure vulnerability deepens, engineering capacity alone no longer explains why water systems fail or how they recover. The study offers a more institutional language for current debates over adaptation and implementation.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 该文对应水治理改革与适应政策设计，并未嵌入某一正式国际谈判文本，但对各类流域和城市水管理制度具有直接参考价值。

The paper speaks to water-governance reform and adaptation policy design; it is not embedded in a formal international negotiation text, but it is directly relevant to basin and urban water-management institutions.

Original: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-026-01972-y

### Who Controls Multilateral Development Finance?

Rebecca Ray · 2021-02-18 · Global Governance: A Review of Multilateralism and International Organizations

#多边治理 #国际治理与多边主义

这篇文章衡量28家多边开发银行（MDB）的治理投票权分布，并据此区分债权人主导、核心借款人主导与互助导向三类制度。它说明，谁掌握治理权，谁就更可能影响发展融资的流向与条件。

The article measures voting-power distributions across 28 multilateral development banks and distinguishes creditor-led, core borrower-led, and mutual aid-oriented governance types. It shows that who holds governance power is closely tied to who can shape the direction and terms of development finance.

**方法论 / Methodology**

作者使用Penrose-Banzhaf与Shapley-Shubik权力指数，并结合成员国在MDB董事会中的相对投票权，对28家MDB的治理结构进行比较测算。

The author uses Penrose-Banzhaf and Shapley-Shubik power indices, together with members’ relative voting power on MDB boards, to compare governance structures across 28 MDBs.

**今日关联 / Today's Connection**

可与本期COP31气候融资僵局一起阅读，因为谈判中反复出现的责任分担、资金来源与融资条件之争，本质上都指向“谁来控制资金、谁来承担成本”。

Read alongside this issue’s COP31 finance deadlock, because the recurring disputes over burden sharing, funding sources, and financing conditions all come back to who controls the money and who bears the costs.

### Legitimacy by Design: Institutional Representation and Public Support for Global Governance

Philipp Bien, Thomas Malang, Matthias Mader · 2026-11-13 · International Studies Quarterly

#国际治理与多边主义 #地缘政治与治理

这篇研究表明，把国会议员纳入国际组织决策，能显著提升公众对全球治理机构的合法性感知。尤其在政治立场更分化、对国际合作更怀疑的人群中，代表性设计更能修复合法性压力，因此可被视为一种可扩展的制度回应。

The study finds that including parliamentarians in international-organization decision-making significantly raises perceived legitimacy. The effect is especially strong among politically diverse or skeptical publics, making representational design a scalable institutional response to legitimacy pressure in global governance.

**方法论 / Methodology**

作者使用预注册联合实验，让受访者评估若干属性不同的虚拟国际组织决策，其中包括是否存在、以及何种类型的议员参与。通过比较这些情境，作者识别代表性安排对合法性感知的影响。

The authors use a pre-registered conjoint experiment in which respondents evaluate hypothetical IO decisions varying across several attributes, including the presence and type of parliamentary involvement. Comparing these scenarios allows them to estimate how representational design affects legitimacy perceptions.

**今日关联 / Today's Connection**

可与COP31破裂后的气候治理改革讨论一起读：当谈判机制难以形成共识时，这篇文章提醒我们，制度设计本身就是合法性的一部分，而不是事后装饰。

Read alongside the post-COP31 debate on climate-governance reform: when negotiation machinery fails to produce consensus, this paper suggests legitimacy is built into institutional design itself, not added afterward.

**研究方向 / Research Directions**

- 更广泛的代表性是否能缓解全球治理中的合法性危机？ (global governance, legitimacy, representation, parliamentarians, conjoint experiment)
  Does broader representation ease legitimacy crises in global governance?
- 哪些制度参与形式最能提升公众支持？ (international organizations, public support, participation, legitimacy, institutional design)
  Which forms of institutional participation most increase public support?

DOI / 原文链接: https://doi.org/10.1093/isq/sqag093
