### 1. Learning Hub Research Grantee Spotlight: Genesis Analytics

Climate Policy Initiative · 2026-08-03

Tags: #综合治理议题 #气候金融 #可持续金融与ESG

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: Climate Policy Initiative 的 Learning Hub 通过委托 Genesis Analytics 研究肯尼亚、尼日利亚和纳米比亚的养老基金与保险资金，指出非洲气候融资瓶颈不只在资本缺口，而在监管规则、会计处理和资本充足率如何把国内机构投资者锁定在主权债务上，从而阻断其进入可再生能源、绿色氢、水安全与韧性交通；文章因此实际上是在为混合融资（blended finance）寻找一套能穿透监管约束的制度设计。

Climate Policy Initiative’s Learning Hub, by commissioning Genesis Analytics to study pension and insurance capital in Kenya, Nigeria, and Namibia, argues that Africa’s climate-finance bottleneck is not only a capital gap but a regulatory one: accounting rules and capital requirements keep domestic institutional investors anchored in sovereign debt rather than renewable energy, green hydrogen, water security, and resilient transport; the piece therefore points toward a blended-finance design that can work through regulatory constraints.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 这篇文章回应的是非洲气候融资长期低配、但本地机构资金规模上升的时点。它把问题从“外部资金不足”推进到“国内金融监管如何改写资产配置”，因此很适合作为今年本地资本动员议程的切口。

The piece responds to the longstanding under-supply of climate finance to Africa at a moment when domestic institutional assets are growing. It shifts the question from external scarcity to how financial regulation can redirect local asset allocation, making it timely for the current domestic-capital mobilization agenda.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 这是气候融资工作流中关于“国内动员”而非“外部输血”的技术性前置议题。

This is a technical upstream issue in the climate-finance workflow, centered on domestic mobilization rather than external inflows.

Original: https://www.climatepolicyinitiative.org/learning-hub-research-grantee-spotlight-genesis-analytics/

### 2. Q&A: Does the world need ‘carbon capture and storage’ to reach net-zero?

Carbon Brief · 2026-08-03

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #气候金融 #能源转型

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: Carbon Brief 以英国为例梳理 CCS 的政策辩论，指出各国净零路线图把碳捕集与封存（carbon capture and storage, CCS）当作难减排部门的关键工具，但其高成本、执行反复和与化石产业的制度连结，使它更像是一个需要严格边界与监督的过渡性基础设施，而不是可自动放大的减排方案；文章隐含的政策含义是，若没有更清晰的公共投资条件、绩效约束与长期储存责任安排，CCS 很容易成为拖延深度减排的制度性借口。

Using the UK as an example, Carbon Brief frames CCS as a central but contested tool in national net-zero pathways: it may be indispensable for hard-to-abate sectors, yet its high costs, repeated delivery slippage, and institutional ties to fossil-fuel interests make it more a transition infrastructure requiring strict boundaries and oversight than a scalable emissions fix. The implied policy lesson is that, without clearer public-investment شروط, performance constraints, and long-term storage liability rules, CCS can become a structural excuse to delay deep decarbonization.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 这篇 Q&A 直接回应了各国净零计划中对 CCS 重新加码、同时争议升温的时点。它把“是否需要 CCS”改写为“谁来为失败风险和长期责任买单”，对当前技术路线争论有即时性。

This Q&A responds to the renewed push for CCS in net-zero plans alongside growing controversy over its costs and credibility. It reframes the issue from whether CCS is needed to who bears failure risk and long-term liability, making it immediately relevant to current technology-pathway debates.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 它位于各国净零战略、工业减排工具箱和基础设施投资审查的交叉点上。

It sits at the intersection of national net-zero strategies, industrial decarbonization toolkits, and infrastructure investment review.

Original: https://www.carbonbrief.org/qa-does-the-world-need-carbon-capture-and-storage-to-reach-net-zero

### Multilateral Climate Governance

Franz Baumann · 2024-08-21 · Global Governance: A Review of Multilateralism and International Organizations

#多边治理 #国际治理与多边主义

这篇文章把《联合国气候变化框架公约》下近半个世纪的谈判放回南北发展分歧与治理僵局中审视，强调气候减缓已经极度紧迫，却仍深受财富分配、发展权与既得利益的结构性约束。它的价值不在于提出单一解决方案，而在于直白地指出：在气候融资、适应与减缓之间，国际社会正在做的是政治选择，而不是技术排序。

This article places nearly half a century of UNFCCC negotiations within the broader conflict between North–South development claims and governance deadlock, stressing that mitigation is now desperately urgent but still constrained by wealth distribution, development rights, and entrenched interests. Its value is not a single solution but a clear reminder that choices between climate finance, adaptation, and mitigation are political, not merely technical.

该文以规范性和历史综述为主，对《联合国气候变化框架公约》谈判进程作概念化总结，并非经验计量研究。

The piece is primarily normative and historical, offering a conceptual synthesis of UNFCCC negotiations rather than an empirical quantitative study.

### Accountability of Networked Climate Governance: The Rise of Transnational Climate Partnerships

Karin Bäckstrand · 2008-08-01 · Global Environmental Politics

#环境治理与气候 #多边治理

这篇文章讨论的是跨国气候伙伴关系如何在国家、企业和非国家行动者之间重组治理权威，以及这种网络化治理如何接受问责。作者指出，伙伴关系并未简单替代国家，而是把规则制定、实施和监测拆分到不同类型的安排中；因此，透明度、监测机制和代表性成为判断其合法性的关键。

This article examines how transnational climate partnerships reorganize governance authority across states, firms, and nonstate actors, and how such networked governance can be held accountable. The key point is that partnerships do not simply replace states; they distribute rule-setting, implementation, and monitoring across different arrangements, making transparency, monitoring, and representation central to legitimacy.

**方法论 / Methodology**

文章通过对跨国气候伙伴关系类型的概念分类与比较分析，评估其透明度、监测和代表性等问责维度。

The article uses conceptual typology and comparative analysis of transnational climate partnerships to assess accountability dimensions such as transparency, monitoring, and representation.

可与本期 E3G 关于南非 JETP 的分析并读，因为两者都在讨论国际伙伴关系如何把公共承诺转化为可问责的执行机制。

Read alongside this issue’s E3G analysis of South Africa’s JETP, since both examine how international partnerships translate public commitments into accountable implementation.
