### 1. Analysis: Wind and solar save UK from gas imports worth £5.9bn during Hormuz crisis

Carbon Brief · 2026-09-30

Tags: #环境治理与气候 #能源转型 #绿色转型

**核心论点 / Core Argument**: 英国在霍尔木兹危机期间凭借风电和光伏创纪录发电，少买了约59亿英镑天然气进口，说明电力系统去气化已从减排目标转化为外部冲击下的宏观稳定器；政策上应把输电、储能与需求侧灵活性当作能源安全基础设施来配置。

Record wind and solar output allowed the UK to avoid roughly £5.9bn in gas imports during the Hormuz crisis, showing that power-sector decarbonization has become a macro-stabilizer under external shocks; policy should treat transmission, storage, and demand-side flexibility as core energy-security infrastructure.

**为什么此刻重要 / Why Now**: 这篇分析直接回应中东战争推高气价的时点，提供了一个可量化的反事实：如果没有可再生能源扩张，英国会面对更高的进口账单和更脆弱的供给链。它也把“能源安全”从化石燃料供应转向系统灵活性。

The analysis responds directly to the Middle East conflict-driven price spike and offers a quantified counterfactual: without renewable build-out, the UK would face a much larger import bill and a more fragile supply chain. It also shifts the meaning of energy security from fossil-fuel supply to system flexibility.

**议程位置 / Agenda Position**: 这是对英国能源安全与电力市场设计讨论的即时介入，也会影响冬季供能与储能投资的政策排序。

This is an immediate intervention in UK energy-security and power-market design debates, with implications for winter supply planning and storage investment priorities.

## 论文研读 / Research Reading

### Public Opinion and the Political Logic of International Climate Pledges

Michaël Aklin, Mark T. Buntaine, Matto Mildenberger · 2026-10-02 · Global Environmental Politics

#环境治理与气候 #多边治理

该文通过跨十个高排放国家、逾一万名受访者的实验表明，巴黎协定之后的公共气候承诺不仅是对外信号，也会被国内公众视为政府政策意图与谈判立场的信息来源；公众通常支持雄心更高的单边承诺，但在高雄心水平下更偏好强调国家间公平的条件性承诺。作者同时发现，伙伴国的条件性承诺并不会自动提高本国公众对国内气候行动的支持，只有当其与雄心较高的无条件承诺并列时，才会提升对向低收入和中等收入国家转移资源的支持。

Using experiments across ten high-emitting countries and more than 10,000 respondents, the article shows that post-Paris public climate pledges are not only external signals but also domestic information about policy intent and bargaining posture. Publics generally prefer ambitious unilateral pledges, but at high ambition levels they favor conditional pledges that emphasize fairness. Counterpart conditional pledges do not automatically raise support for domestic action, and they increase support for transfers to low- and middle-income countries only when paired with ambitious unconditional pledges.

**方法论 / Methodology**

作者在十个高排放国家实施了三组实验，并以超过一万名受访者检验不同类型气候承诺对公众偏好的影响。

The authors ran three experiments across ten high-emitting countries with more than 10,000 respondents to test how different pledge types shape public preferences.

**今日关联 / Today's Connection**

本期《A Supreme Court Battle Over Climate Change Begins》显示，国内责任分配正从承诺政治转入司法争议，可与本文关于公众如何读取气候承诺的信息机制对照阅读。

This issue’s “A Supreme Court Battle Over Climate Change Begins” shows domestic responsibility allocation moving from pledge politics into litigation, making it a useful counterpart to this article’s account of how publics read climate commitments.

**研究方向 / Research Directions**

- 条件承诺何时更有效？ (climate pledges, public opinion, conditional commitments, experiments)
  When do conditional pledges become more persuasive domestically?
- 承诺如何影响转移支持？ (international transfers, climate finance, pledge politics, public opinion)
  How do pledges shape support for international climate transfers?

### Beyond Membership

Elise Doumergue, Hussam Hussein · 2026-09-30 · Global Governance: A Review of Multilateralism and International Organizations

#多边治理 #国际治理与多边主义

文章解释国家为何在缺乏投票权的情况下仍寻求区域国际组织的观察员身份：这种身份可作为被承认和合作意图的信号、进入组织社会网络以获取信息与影响的通道，以及提升地位的象征资源。作者通过二十一位实务界访谈、官方文件与外交通信构建原创数据，显示观察员身份是一个被低估但具有实用、关系和象征价值的外交工具。

The article explains why states pursue observer status in regional international organizations despite lacking voting rights: it can signal recognition and cooperation intent, provide access to social networks for information and influence, and enhance prestige through collective recognition. Using twenty-one practitioner interviews, official documents, and diplomatic communications, the authors show that observer status is an underexamined but strategically useful diplomatic tool with practical, relational, and symbolic value.

**方法论 / Methodology**

作者使用原创经验数据，结合二十一位实务界访谈、官方文件与外交通信进行分析。

The authors analyze original empirical material combining twenty-one practitioner interviews, official documents, and diplomatic communications.

This issue’s “The Guardian view on Lula v the Bolsonaros: the decision of Brazilian voters will affect us all | Editorial” reminds us that domestic politics outside regional and global bodies can reshape cooperation incentives, a useful complement to this article’s discussion of observer status as diplomacy.
